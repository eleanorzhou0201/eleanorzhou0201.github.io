About

Hello! I am Lizhuo Zhou, and I also go by Eleanor. I recently completed my M.Sc. in Statistics and Data Science at Northwestern University. Previously, I received my B.Sc. in Statistics from Fudan University.

My research interests lie at the intersection of statistical inference, optimal transport, and machine learning . I am particularly interested in developing mathematically grounded and interpretable methods for complex, high-dimensional, and dynamically evolving systems.

My current work includes stochastic phase-field models for longitudinal medical imaging, personalized sequential Bayesian inference, and theoretical connections between Wasserstein gradient flows and large deviations.

I enjoy research that connects rigorous statistical theory with meaningful scientific applications. I am always happy to discuss interesting problems and potential research collaborations.

“I am interested in the mathematical structures behind learning, uncertainty, and evolving systems—and in turning those structures into useful statistical methods.”
